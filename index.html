Support

Find answers to the most common questions about Easybank.

How do I open an account?

Click "Request Invite" and follow the steps. It takes less than 5 minutes.

Are there any monthly fees?

No. Easybank is completely free for personal accounts.

How do I contact a human?

Use the contact form or email support@easybank.com.

Yes. Your deposits are insured up to $250,000 by the FDIC.
